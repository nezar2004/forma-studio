04 / Library management

Librarian

One library system, two distinct roles.

A university team project with separate entry points for students and librarians, plus tools for managing books, members, and loans.

Open Librarian Open the catalog

The design challenge

Students need an entry point for library services, while librarians need tools to maintain the catalog and manage lending. The homepage has to make both paths easy to find.

The design decision

The redesign puts catalog search on the homepage so any visitor can find a book and check availability or shelf location. Student and librarian accounts have separate entry points.

01

Student entry

The student sign-in asks for a university email. The public screen offers account registration and a password reset option.

02

Librarian entry

Librarians have a separate sign-in. The project presents catalog management, member records, borrowing, returns, and reporting as staff workflows.
