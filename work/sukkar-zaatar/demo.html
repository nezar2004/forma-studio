Good to know

Questions, answered.

Explore how this interactive university project works before placing your demo order.

Can I place a real order?

This version is an academic design demonstration. Orders are saved only in your current browser, and no real payment or bakery delivery happens.

Can I save favourite products?

Yes! Tap the heart on a product card, then open “My favourites”. Your picks are stored locally in this browser.

Can I change my order?

You can edit quantities or remove products from the bag before placing a demo order. Afterwards, the demo admin dashboard can update its status.

Does the bakery support pickup?

The demo checkout includes delivery and pickup options. These do not represent a real business fulfilment service.
